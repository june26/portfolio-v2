Lisbon, Portugal

I build interfaces that feel fast, quiet and inevitable.

Eight years on the frontend — first at an agency building campaign sites, then in-house on a SaaS product with 200,000 monthly users, and since 2021 as an independent engineer working with small product teams.

Most of what I do is unglamorous and load-bearing: typed component layers, design tokens that survive a rebrand, a loading state that doesn’t flash, a bundle that stays under budget. I care about how the thing feels on a three-year-old phone, not on a benchmark machine.
